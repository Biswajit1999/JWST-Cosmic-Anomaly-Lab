# Research record

Biswajit Jana leads the JWST Cosmic Anomaly Lab. The reports link to
the numerical methods, input products and figures for each analysis.
Observation and catalogue credits belong to the teams identified there.

## Data and measurements

The [download manifest](../data/manifests/downloads.json) identifies
archive products and records their checksums. The [experiment records](../experiments/)
give the commands and the scope of each analysis. Observed figures are
rendered from released telescope images or spectra. Synthetic signals
used in recovery tests are identified in the reports and result tables.

## Research and manuscript preparation

OpenAI Codex has assisted with software, archive queries, running numerical
analyses, literature checks, figures prepared from measured data, and writing.
The reported redshifts, fluxes, upper limits and recovery rates come from the
archive products and documented numerical calculations. Manuscript
acknowledgments describe the significant assistance used for each paper.
Biswajit Jana is responsible for reviewing and approving the work.
