# Observed image and spectrum gallery

20 observed source evidence sheets; 18 three-band RGB cutouts. These are telescope data, not illustrations or simulated sources.

![Observed source comparison](../figures/observed_image_comparison.png)

Open an individual sheet below for the three filter images, the 1D spectrum with errors, the continuum windows, and the actual 2D spectral image.

**No source here is established as a black-hole star by this analysis.** Read the [physical-test status](black_hole_star_status.md) and [rendering methods and limitations](../docs/visual_evidence.md).
